# GroLocal 🌱

## Grow local. Share culture. Connect.

GroLocal is a community marketplace connecting people through home-grown produce, plants, gardening knowledge and cultural stories.

### Features

- 🌱 Community marketplace for home-grown produce and plants
- 👩‍🌾 Gardener profiles and gardens
- 🌏 Cultural and family stories connected to plants
- 💬 Community discussions and knowledge sharing
- 📍 Discover gardens and growers in your community
